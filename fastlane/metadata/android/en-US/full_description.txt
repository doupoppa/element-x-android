Freedom to communicate on your own terms.

For individuals and communities: private communication between family, friends, hobby groups, and teams.

GTalk gives you fast, secure, and private instant messaging and video calls built on Matrix, the open standard for real-time communication.

Stay in touch with friends, family, and communities with:
 • Real-time messaging and video calls
 • Public rooms for open group communication
 • Private rooms for closed group communication
 • Rich messaging features: emoji reactions, replies, polls, pinned messages, and more
 • Video calling while browsing messages
 • Interoperability with other Matrix-based apps

<b>Privacy-first</b>
Unlike many mainstream messengers, GTalk is designed to respect your data and your conversations.

<b>Own your conversations</b>
Choose where to host your data: on a public server, a private server, or your own domain.

<b>Communicate in real time, all the time</b>
Stay in sync across your devices with a consistent messaging experience.

The application requires the android.permission.REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES permission to enable installation of applications received as attachments.

The application requires the USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT permission to ensure users can receive call notifications effectively, even when devices are locked.
